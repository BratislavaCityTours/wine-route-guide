PHOTOS FOR THE AUDIO GUIDE
==========================

Drop your own photos in THIS folder. They appear automatically on the cards.
No file editing needed - just use the exact file names below.

Everything here is OPTIONAL. Any photo you don't add simply shows an
elegant burgundy placeholder instead. Nothing breaks.

USE YOUR OWN PHOTOS. Do not download pictures from Google Images -
they are usually copyrighted and you would be publishing them commercially.

--- THE 7 MAIN STOP PHOTOS (do these first - biggest visual impact) ---

  welcome.jpg          Bratislava / the vehicle / vineyards
  castle-arrival.jpg   Red Stone Castle from outside
  castle-museum.jpg    Castle interior or courtyard
  modra.jpg            Modra main square or majolica pottery
  pezinok.jpg          Pezinok town centre or vineyards
  svaty-jur.jpg        Svaty Jur / the wine tasting
  thank-you.jpg        Wine glasses / your team / logo

--- OPTIONAL: individual track photos ---

Name the file after the track id, for example:
  sala-terrena.jpg
  knights-hall.jpg
  pezinok-history.jpg

The track ids are listed in content/en.json.

--- RECOMMENDED FORMAT ---

  Landscape (16:9), about 1200 x 675 pixels
  Save as .jpg, aim for under 300 KB each so the app stays fast
